═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  VALIDATION RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

• Date: YYYY-MM-DD format
• Plant Code: Must match existing plants
• Status: Operating, Maintenance, Shutdown, or Standby
• Available ≤ Installed Capacity
• Operating Units ≤ Total Units
• Percentages: 0-100
