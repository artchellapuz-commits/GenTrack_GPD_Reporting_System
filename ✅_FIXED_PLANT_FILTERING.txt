============================================================
FIXED: PLANT FILTERING ISSUE
============================================================

THE PROBLEM:
The backend wasn't recognizing the plant_code[] parameter
sent by axios. Django was looking for 'plant_code' but
axios was sending 'plant_code[]' (with brackets).

THE FIX:
Modified the backend to handle BOTH formats:
- plant_code[] (sent by axios when passing an array)
- plant_code (standard format)
