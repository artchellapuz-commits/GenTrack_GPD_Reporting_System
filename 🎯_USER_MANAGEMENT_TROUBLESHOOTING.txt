═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
   🔍 WHY IT'S NOT SHOWING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The User Management link is ALREADY in the code at:
frontend/src/components/Sidebar.vue (line 65-68)

But it's wrapped in a condition:
  <template v-if="isAdminUser">

The isAdminUser variable checks if:
1. user.is_staff === true, OR
2. user.profile.role === 'ADMIN'

If your localStorage doesn't have this data, the link won't show.
