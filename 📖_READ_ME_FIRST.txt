THE PROBLEM:
  You have a Plant Status report, but your system needs daily generation data.

THE DIFFERENCE:
  Plant Status = Current snapshot (what's happening NOW)
  Generation Data = Historical records (what happened OVER TIME)

THE SOLUTION:
  1. Understand the difference (read the guides)
  2. Show supervisor what you need (use the Excel template)
  3. Find the data source (SCADA, database, Excel, logbooks)
  4. Get sample data (test the system)
  5. Establish regular updates (ongoing process)
