In the terminal where the frontend is running:

Press: Ctrl + C

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

cd frontend

Wait for it to say "Compiled successfully"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

In your browser:

Press: Ctrl + Shift + R (Windows/Linux)
Or: Cmd + Shift + R (Mac)

This clears the cache and reloads the page.

   - Select "Cached images and files"
   - Click "Clear data"

   - Ctrl+Shift+N (Chrome)
   - Go to http://localhost:8080
   - Login and check sidebar

   - Press F12
