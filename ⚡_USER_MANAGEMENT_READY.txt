═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
   👥 USER ROLES EXPLAINED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

VIEWER:
- View reports and dashboards only
- No upload or modification rights

OPERATOR:
- All Viewer permissions
- Upload Excel files
- Submit water nominations
- Cannot approve nominations

MANAGER:
- All Operator permissions
- Approve water nominations
- Manage team data

ADMIN:
- Full system access
- Manage users
- Access admin panel
- View audit logs
