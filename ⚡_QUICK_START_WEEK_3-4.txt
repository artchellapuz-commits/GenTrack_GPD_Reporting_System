🔍 FEATURE 2: ADVANCED FILTERING
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

WHERE: View Reports page (http://localhost:8080/view-reports)

WHAT YOU'LL SEE:
  - Purple gradient panel: "Advanced Filters"
  - Badge showing active filter count
  - Click to expand/collapse

HOW TO TEST:
  1. Click "Advanced Filters" header to expand

  7. Click "Save Current" to save preset
  8. Load preset later with one click

FEATURES:

  ✅ Numeric ranges (Generation, Capacity Factor)
