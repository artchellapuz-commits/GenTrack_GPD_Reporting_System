REPORT FORMAT:
==============

Generated PSR reports include:
- NPC/GPD header with logo
- Report date and time
- Plant status table with:
  * Plant name and units
  * Rated capacity
  * Dependable capacity
  * Load at report time
  * Remarks
- IPP section (STEAG units)
- Total NPC and Total IPP rows
- Forecasted load (yellow row)
- Pie chart (generation distribution)
- Bar chart (capacity comparison)
- Notes section
- Lake elevations
- Signature footer
